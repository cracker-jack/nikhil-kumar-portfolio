Privacy policy

What happens to your information when you contact me or book a direct session through this portfolio.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Who is responsible

This website and its direct-session booking service are operated by Nikhil Kumar. For questions about your information, email kumarnikhil374@gmail.com.

Information used for bookings

When you request a direct session, the booking service receives your name, email address, selected service, date and time. It stores booking and payment status, references and any calendar-event details needed to manage the session. If you email me directly, I receive the information you choose to send.

Checking available times uses access to my calendars to determine busy periods; the public site receives available slots, not private calendar events or their details. Google OAuth access is granted by me, not by visitors.

How this information is used

I use booking information to arrange and manage your session, verify payment, send a Google Calendar invitation, resolve booking or payment problems, respond to your messages and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. The site does not currently send WhatsApp confirmations or collect a WhatsApp number for bookings.

Services involved

Razorpay processes direct-session payments through its checkout; I receive payment and order status, not your full card details. Google Calendar checks availability and sends invitations to the email you provide. Google Cloud Run hosts the booking API, and Google Cloud Firestore stores booking records. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. If you choose to book through Topmate instead, you leave this site and Topmate's own policies apply.

Storage and your choices

Booking records are retained to manage sessions, handle payment issues and satisfy applicable legal or accounting requirements. No fixed automatic deletion period is configured for the booking records. You can email me to ask what personal information I hold, request correction or deletion where possible, or ask a question about how it is handled. Some records may need to be kept for legal or payment-related reasons.

This website does not use advertising trackers or require an account to browse. External services you choose to use may apply their own cookies or technical logs. Information processed by these services may be handled outside your country.

Changes to this policy

If the booking service or data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. For direct-session conditions, see the terms of service.
